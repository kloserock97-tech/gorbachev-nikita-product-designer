The grass from the first screen of this site as a demo of its own. The graphics card draws 380,000 real blades with one command (instancing), and the processor rests.

Wind comes in gusts, grass grows in tufts, and the cursor leaves a trail like a hand on a carpet. The graphics card does all of it (in a vertex shader).

To keep far grass from shimmering, a blade is never thinner than a pixel and gets wider with distance. You can play with everything on a panel, and the link keeps your settings.
